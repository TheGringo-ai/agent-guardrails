## Mechanics

- **Comment, don't submit,** for the first several days.
- Reply to a *specific person* rather than posting a top-level comment. Replies get read;
  top-level comments on a 300-comment thread do not.
- Length: 3–8 sentences. HN rewards density, not volume.
- No exclamation marks, no "Great post!", no hedging preamble. Open with the claim.
- Concede anything that is genuinely arguable. On HN, conceding a point raises your
  standing; defending a weak one destroys it.
- If someone disagrees well, say so and update. That single move earns more credibility
  than being right.
